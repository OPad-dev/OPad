# Hardware review: PCBs (`hardware/pcb/V1`) and case (`hardware/3d/custom_case/V1`)

- Date: 2026-09-24
- Base: `main` @ `f78bfbd`, clean tree
- Tools: KiCad 10.0.6 (`kicad-cli`, `pcbnew` Python, KiCad MCP server), OpenSCAD 2021.01, trimesh. Waveshare references: the ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2 schematic PDF, the 2024-11-08 mechanical drawing/STEP, and the wiki pinout image.
- Method: ERC + DRC with schematic parity and zone refill on all three boards; pad-level comparison of board footprints against `lib/`; production files regenerated and diffed against the committed ones; carrier pin map checked against the Waveshare schematic and back-view pinout; the case checked for interference against a Waveshare + carrier stack built from Waveshare's drawing, and against the input module, switches and keycaps.
- Verification: every finding below was reproduced *(verified)*. Case clearances come from a box model of the stack, so they are accurate to the dimensions quoted, not to the last 0.1 mm.
- Status legend: `open` / `fixed` / `wontfix` / `invalid`

Findings are ranked most severe first.

## 1. The Waveshare + carrier stack collides with the case — `fixed` *(verified)*

**Fixed in case V1.1** (`hardware/3d/custom_case/V1.1`): 16° screen pod with the stack 0.6 mm above the floor, pillars replaced by posts under the carrier holes, and a fit check (`tools/check_fit.py`) that passes. V1 is kept unchanged.

**File:** `hardware/3d/custom_case/V1/osupad_enclosure.scad:332`
**Category:** mechanical

`hardware/pcb/V1/README.md` already says "the V1 case does not fit this stack". The 3D intersection shows exactly where:

| Part of the stack | Hits | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carrier PCB (left arm) | rear-left pillar (18.6, 73.5), h 19.2 | 27 mm³ overlap |
| J_MOD on the carrier bottom | same pillar | 2 mm³ overlap |
| Carrier lower long edge | case floor | 0.26 mm clearance |
| Socket pin tails, clipped to 1 mm | case floor | touching (−0.07 mm) |

The four "screen support pillars" are also not on the Waveshare holes. The holes sit at case X = 38 ± 21.1 = 16.9 / 59.1, while the pillars are at X = 18.6 / 60.8, 1.7 mm off. Nothing else in the case holds the carrier.

**Suggested fix:**
- Delete the pillars.
- Add four M2 standoffs from the bottom plate, square to the deck, at the Waveshare hole positions (deck-local ±21.1 × ±14.5 mm). Size them to the carrier bottom, 19.8 mm below the glass front.
- Raise the screen deck by at least 3 mm so the carrier edge and the solder tails clear the floor.

The front pillars (Y 44.5) clear everything. USB-C access was checked too and does **not** need changing: the side channel of the screen cutout passes a 12 × 6.5 mm plug overmold.

## 2. `he_input_v1.kicad_sch` cannot be opened by any KiCad — `open` *(verified)*

**File:** `hardware/pcb/V1/scripts/generate_he_kicad.py:578`
**Category:** correctness

`write_schematic` writes each `NOTES` string with `'(text "%s" …'` without escaping it. The note at line 549 contains `"Hall Effect module V1"` in double quotes, which ends the string early. `kicad-cli sch erc` fails with `Expecting '(' … line 875, offset 85`, and eeschema won't open the file.

**Knock-on:** `export_production.py` processes MX → HE → Carrier. `run()` calls `sys.exit` when the HE ERC fails, so **the carrier is never exported** by a full run.

**Suggested fix:** escape `"` and `\` in every string the generator writes (notes, property values), or drop the inner quotes. Then regenerate.

## 3. One broken footprint disables the whole `osupad` footprint library in KiCad 10 — `open` *(verified)*

**File:** `hardware/pcb/V1/lib/osupad.pretty/SW_MX_Hall_PlateMount.kicad_mod:59`
**Category:** correctness

The `fp_rect` uses the schematic-style fill syntax `(fill (type none))`, which the footprint parser rejects. KiCad then drops the entire library:
- every footprint on all three boards gets a `lib_footprint_issues` "library 'osupad' is not enabled" warning;
- ERC reports `footprint_link_issues` for every symbol;
- "Update footprints from library" does not work in the GUI.

No board uses this footprint. Replacing the fill with `(fill no)` fixes it; that was confirmed by loading the patched library.

**Suggested fix:** use `(fill no)`. Since the file is hand-drawn and not produced by `gen_library.py`, also add a load test for the library, e.g. `kicad-cli fp export svg` over `osupad.pretty`.

## 4. `export_production.py` cannot pass on a clean KiCad 10 setup; the README's "zero warnings" status no longer holds — `open` *(verified)*

**File:** `hardware/pcb/V1/scripts/export_production.py:33`
**Category:** tooling

`ACCEPTED_WARNINGS` is empty, so any warning stops a board. Even with #2 and #3 fixed, MX and the carrier still fail, because commit `4dabf93` regenerated `lib/` after the boards were last checked (the committed `drc.json` files date from 2026-09-14):

| Board | ERC `lib_symbol_mismatch` | DRC `lib_footprint_mismatch` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MX | 8 | 8 |
| Carrier | 3 | 7 |
| HE | 8 | 13 |

The drift is **cosmetic**. A pad-by-pad comparison (position, size, drill, shape, type, angle) shows every board footprint identical to its library version, so the silkscreen, courtyard and text differ but the copper and drills don't.

The committed Gerbers and drill files are current: MX and the carrier regenerate byte-identical apart from the job file's dates, and HE apart from timestamps.

**Suggested fix:**
1. Update symbols and footprints from the library on MX and the carrier, or re-run `generate_boards.py`.
2. Re-run `export_production.py`.
3. Confirm the copper and drill Gerbers are unchanged.

## 5. HE board: first KiCad DRC/ERC run (38 DRC, 10 ERC) — `open` *(verified)*

**File:** `hardware/pcb/V1/scripts/he_board.py`
**Category:** correctness / cosmetic

Checked on a copy with #2 patched. `he_board.py`'s own DRC and `verify_he_gerbers.py` both report clean, and KiCad finds 0 unconnected pads and 0 clearance errors. The remaining issues don't affect the copper:

- **6× `courtyards_overlap` (error).** SW1/SW2 (`SW_MX_Hall_PlateMount_SolidCentre`, the switch-position markers) are placed on **B.Cu**, so their 14.4 mm switch courtyard lands on B.CrtYd over U1/U2, C2/C3 and TP2/TP3. The switches are on the top side: place these markers on F.Cu. The holes are NPTH and symmetric, so the drill file is unchanged.
- **2× `net_conflict` (parity).** J1's MP pads are GND on the board, but the `Conn_01x08` symbol has no MP pin. MX and the carrier leave MP unconnected, so either do the same here or add an MP pin to the symbol.
- **2× ERC `power_pin_not_driven`** on U1/U2 VCC: add a `PWR_FLAG` on 3V3 and GND.
- **11× `text_height`:** 0.7 mm silkscreen text, below the board's 0.8 mm minimum and JLCPCB's recommended ~1.0 mm.
- **6× `nonmirrored_text_on_back_layer`** (J1, C3, R2 fields), and **2× `silk_over_copper`**: U2's outline runs over pad 1 of U1/U2.

## 6. The glass pocket has 0.09 mm clearance per side — `fixed` *(verified)*

**Fixed in case V1.1:** 0.3 mm per side, sized from the STEP glass, with the pocket depth set so the glass sits flush on 0.15 mm tape.

**File:** `hardware/3d/custom_case/V1/osupad_enclosure.scad:22`
**Category:** mechanical

The pocket is 59.4 × 37.7 mm. Waveshare's drawing gives the lens as 58.8 × 37.1, but the STEP glass measures **59.22 × 37.52** (as `hardware/3d/README.md` notes), leaving 0.09 mm per side. FDM pockets usually print 0.1–0.2 mm undersize, so the glass will probably not go in. The ledge that holds the glass on its long sides is only (37.52 − 36.2) / 2 = 0.66 mm wide.

**Suggested fix:** `LCD_LENS_X/Y` = 59.8 / 38.1 (+0.3 mm per side), and hold the glass on the ledge with thin double-sided tape.

## 7. The MX clip notches are cut above the deck, so they do nothing — `fixed` *(verified)*

**Fixed in case V1.1:** the notches were removed. The 16 × 16 mm relief under the 1.5 mm plate clears the clips.

**File:** `hardware/3d/custom_case/V1/osupad_enclosure.scad:100`
**Category:** mechanical / cleanup

The two 14.6 × 3.8 notches span z −0.1…+1.6 relative to `H_FLAT`, and the deck's top surface *is* `H_FLAT`, so only 0.1 mm is removed. The switches still clip in: the plate is 1.5 mm (z 13.5–15), and the 16 × 16 mm relief below it clears the clips.

**Suggested fix:** either move the notches to z −1.6…+0.1 (the usual clip notch) or delete them.

## 8. Every module pin is also a camera signal — `open` *(verified, docs)*

**File:** `hardware/pcb/V1/README.md` (carrier section)
**Category:** documentation

Per the Waveshare schematic, the connector's GPIOs double as camera lines:

| GPIO | Camera signal |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | CAM_D7 |
| 4 | CAM_HREF |
| 6 | CAM_VSYNC |
| 7 | CAM_D6 |
| 8 | CAM_XCLK |
| 10 | CAM_D5 |

The README says the camera FPC "stays reachable", but a camera and an input module can't be used together. A camera driving XCLK on GPIO8 would also corrupt the module ID reading.

**Suggested fix:** say so in the README.

## 9. The reserved "Hall SPI" ID sits 50 mV from the HE decode threshold — `open` *(verified, future)*

**File:** `firmware/boards/waveshare_esp32s3_touch_lcd_2/board.c:269`
**Category:** design margin

The README reserves 100k/100k = 1.65 V for an SPI module. The firmware decodes HE as 0.65–1.6 V, so a 1.65 V module reads 50 mV above the cut. That is within 1 % divider tolerance plus ADC calibration error, so it could be taken for HE.

**Suggested fix:** move the cut to about 1.35 V before an SPI module exists.

## Checked and not reported

- **Carrier ↔ Waveshare pin map.**
  - P1-1/2/3 = IO2/4/6, P1-8/9/10 = ID (GPIO8) / IN2 (GPIO7) / IN1 (GPIO10), P2-1/2 = 3V3/GND. These match the Waveshare schematic's P1/P2 symbols.
  - Header orientation: Waveshare's back view has P1 on the left and P2 on the right, pin 1 away from USB. The carrier's top view is correctly its mirror image.
- **Carrier mechanics against Waveshare's 2024-11-08 drawing:** outline 35.0 × 48.2, holes 3.00 mm from the edges on a 29.00 × 42.20 mm pattern, header rows 30.48 mm apart and centred (pin 1 7.59 mm from the non-USB edge).
- **Firmware `board_pins.h` against the schematic:** LCD SPI 39/38, DC 42, CS 45, backlight 1, I2C 48/47, touch INT 46. The LCD reset is on IO0 (BOOT) and is correctly left `NC`.
- **ERC/DRC on MX and the carrier:** 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 parity issues. Apart from #3/#4, no other warnings.
- **MX Kailh footprint:**
  - Pin holes at (−3.81, −2.54) and (2.54, −5.08), pads at (−7.085, −2.54) and (5.842, −5.08), size 2.55 × 2.5, 4.0 mm centre and 1.75 mm pegs. This is the standard MX hot-swap pattern, with the pins toward the rear.
  - J1 (SM08B) pads and MP match the JST land pattern.
- **Module ID:** MX 100k/10k = 0.30 V (0.25 V with the pull-down) and HE 100k/47k = 1.06 V (0.62 V with the pull-down), both inside the firmware's 120 / 650 / 1600 mV cuts. The LCSC numbers match the README (C25819 = 47k, C266128 = DRV5055A3).
- **HE geometry:** U1/U2 exactly on the key centres (case 28.475 / 47.525, 16.5), rotation 180°, and DRV5055 SOT-23 pads matching the library. The SPI-reserved pins go to TP1–TP3.
- **Production files:** Gerbers and drills for all three boards regenerate identical to the committed zips (timestamps only). The HE BOM and CPL are identical.
- **Case STLs:** regenerated from the `.scad` with the same volume and bounds as the committed files, and watertight.
- **Input module in the case:**
  - The MX PCB rests exactly on the M2 bosses (top 10.4 mm), with its top 5.0 mm under the plate top.
  - Switch housings, keycaps at full travel, the Kailh sockets, J1 and a mated JST plug all clear the case and the bottom plate.
  - Key centres and boss positions match the PCB.
- **USB-C:** the port's Y matches the receptacle (62.0). A plug shell, a slim 10.5 × 5.5 overmold and a typical 12 × 6.5 overmold all clear the top case.
